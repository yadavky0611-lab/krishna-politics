Indian Politics

Learn how India's political system works.

Politics is how people and institutions make collective decisions. India is a democratic republic with a parliamentary system. Citizens choose representatives, governments make public decisions, legislatures debate laws, and courts interpret laws and constitutional principles.

🏛️

Political Parties

Understand parties, ideas and manifestos.

A political party organises people around political ideas and seeks public support. To compare parties, read their published manifestos, examine records and outcomes, and verify claims with multiple credible sources. A promise, an opinion and a verified result are not the same thing.

📜

Government Policies

Explore policies, schemes and laws.

Public policy is a government plan to address an issue such as education, transport, employment or the environment. When studying a scheme, check the official department, eligibility, benefits, dates and available evidence about implementation.

🗳️

Elections

Learn about voting and the election process.

Elections allow eligible voters to choose representatives. A constituency is an area represented by an elected member; a candidate contests an election; a manifesto describes a party's programme. Check official election announcements for current dates and rules.

📘

Indian Constitution

Learn about rights, duties and institutions.

The Constitution is India's supreme legal framework. It defines institutions, distributes powers and protects rights. Useful topics include the Preamble, Fundamental Rights, Fundamental Duties, Directive Principles and the relationship between Union and state governments.

🏢

Parliament

Understand Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha and laws.

India's Union Parliament consists of the President and two Houses: Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha. Members debate public issues, scrutinise government work and consider legislation. The procedure for a bill depends on its type and constitutional rules.
